## 1. Summary

A partner wanted a sample which showed what the deliverables would look like, so this work
focused more on ensuring that everything in the folder was correct, documented, and usable
without calling us than on generating variant calls. The following checks identified 4 issues
before release:

- The SV benchmarking step hung for 28 minutes when the default alignment tool was used.
- Per-haplotype methylation files were produced but left out of the folder.
- The summary said it covered "50 bp and larger" SVs, while the VCFs contained 66 smaller calls.
- While HG002 DNA was derived from a cell line, its methylation patterns were described in
  the summary as those of normal cells.

All 4 items were corrected, and the folder was regenerated from scratch before it was sent. For
this region, the total task time for the entire module was 266 s (according to `qc.json`). This
run was completed in less than 5 minutes.

## 4. Problems found before release

### The SV benchmark hung on its default aligner

`truvari refine` realigns regions where variant calls disagree with the benchmark. This allows
the same event to be counted as a match even when it is represented in two different ways. With
its default aligner (POA), the Sniffles run printed

```text
[SIMDMalloc] posix_memalign fail! Size: 34359738368, Error: 12
```

That is a failed request for 32 GiB. CPU usage then remained at 0% for 28 minutes until
the run was terminated. The pbsv run targeted 3 regions for refine while Sniffles targeted 5
regions, and the pbsv run completed within seconds. This task currently uses MAFFT bundled in the
same truvari image and applies a timeout of 30 minutes when running refine. This ensures that any
future hang becomes a failure. Using MAFFT, the entire Sniffles benchmarking task including refine
took about 130 s (128.5 s for the last run, from `qc.json`).

Refinement affects the values. Before refinement, recall was 0.865 for pbsv and 0.846 for
Sniffles. After refinement, recall increased to 0.923 and 0.904, respectively, while precision
remained unchanged. Since partners who run only `truvari bench` will obtain lower values, we
included both sets of values in the summary.

### Per-haplotype methylation was produced and then dropped

Among the 14,151 reads, 13,404 (95%) carried the `HP` haplotype tag. pb-CpG-tools wrote the
hap1 and hap2 files to the same directory as the merged file. Previously, the workflow
collected only the merged file. This hand-off now includes the haplotype files and their
respective indexes, and these are also listed in the summary:

- hap1 had 62,589 CpGs and hap2 had 62,878 CpGs. Mean methylation rates were 66.5% and 66.8%, respectively.
- Among 55,786 CpG sites with 10 or more reads in each haplotype, 4,925 (8.8%) showed a
  difference of 50 percentage points or more.

This proportion is reported without interpretation. We confirmed that it was not solely due to
sampling noise from halving the read count per haplotype. Even among CpGs with 25 or more reads on
each haplotype, the proportion remained at 8.3%. Furthermore, 4,357 of 4,925 sites were within 1
kb of another site in this set, indicating regional clustering of these differences. Whether these
regions reflect imprinting, the cell line, or other factors was not examined. Additionally, the
summary noted that haplotype numbering remains consistent only within the same phasing block.

### The summary said "50 bp and larger" but the files did not

pbsv used a default minimum length of 20 bp (`--min-sv-length 20`) and reported 64 events shorter
than 50 bp. Sniffles reported 2 deletions which were 46 bp and 49 bp long. These 66 calls
are in the delivered VCFs, but the benchmark contains only events of 50 bp and larger.
Since truvari compares calls of at least 30 bp (`--sizefilt 30`), calls slightly shorter than 50
bp can still match benchmark events. Calls shorter than 30 bp are not scored. The heading
now says only "Structural variants". A sentence below the size table explains how many calls
were shorter than 50 bp and how they were handled during benchmarking.

### "Expected of normal cells"

HG002 DNA comes from a lymphoblastoid cell line, and methylation at some loci differs from that in
blood or tissue. The summary currently states that a 2-peak pattern is typical of human DNA and
includes this caveat.

## 5. What this delivery does not show

- **Accuracy for repeats and methylation.** SV values are based on benchmarking. No TRGT truth set
  or CpG truth set is available for this region. Therefore, these checks only show that all
  results are present and appear reasonable.
- **Anything beyond one sample and 5 Mb.** There is substantial uncertainty in the rates calculated from 52 benchmark SVs.
- **Interpretation.** Allele lengths and motif counts are presented for 2 disease-associated
  repeat loci, together with read-level plots. Whether these lengths fall within the normal range
  is left for clinical-grade assessment.
